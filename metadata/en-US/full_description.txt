# تعويذ وارو ڪيبورڊ (Taveez Waro Keyboard)

**Taveez Waro Keyboard** is a fast gesture and wheel-based Android keyboard for **Sindhi (سنڌي)** and **English**, inspired by the 8Pen concept.

### ✨ Features
- **4-Concentric-Ring Frequency Layouts**: All letters ordered by authentic language frequency, placing the top 8 most frequent letters directly on the first ring next to the center circle.
- **Full Support for Sindhi and English**: Easy language cycling directly from the sidebar globe button or settings.
- **Smart Gestures & Quick Shift**: Intuitive gesture paths for capitals, airabs (اعراب), punctuation, cursor movements, and text replacement.
- **Offline & Private**: Completely offline with zero data collection.
